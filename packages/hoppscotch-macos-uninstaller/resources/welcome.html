ℹ️ Why does the button say "Install"? macOS always labels this button Install — even for uninstallers. It is a system label that cannot be renamed. Clicking Install will permanently remove Hoppscotch from this Mac. Nothing is being installed.

Remove Hoppscotch from this Mac

Clicking Install will remove Hoppscotch from your Mac.

✅ Your collections and data are preserved Your saved collections, environments, and request history are stored in ~/Library/Application Support/io.hoppscotch.desktop and will not be deleted. If you reinstall Hoppscotch, they will be automatically restored. To permanently remove all data, delete that folder manually after uninstalling.
⚠️ This action cannot be undone. The app and its caches will be permanently removed. Your collections are safe — see the note above.
